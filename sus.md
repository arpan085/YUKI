# Safe and sister-friendly birthday wording

This version keeps the birthday page warm, appreciative, and playful without sounding too intense or jealousy-triggering.

## Best lines to keep

- "Dada lai dhanyabad van vaii."
- "You are like a sister to me, and I do care."
- "We all care, and we are so happy to celebrate you today."
- "Happy Birthday, sis."
- "You are like a sister to us, and we are all happy to celebrate your beautiful day."
- "Once again, happy birthday, sis."

## Safe birthday-page tone

- cheerful
- affectionate
- family-like
- respectful
- supportive
- clearly friendly and celebratory

## What to avoid

Avoid lines that sound too intense, too emotionally loaded, or too personal for a mutual friend’s birthday page. The site should feel warm and celebratory, not like a romantic or possessive message.

## Final version

The birthday website should read as:

- a celebration of Yudina
- a respectful sister-like bond
- genuine care from everyone
- light laughter and good energy
- a happy, safe, and affectionate birthday message
